FastGate: autonomous drone through 4 gates

A DJI Tello flies a 4-gate course on its own and lands at a stop sign, using a YOLOv8 detector, a 6-state machine and PID alignment in ROS 2.

The mission

Fly a small DJI Tello through four gates in order with no human input, then land precisely when a stop sign appears. The only sensor is the drone's own forward camera.

How it works

A YOLOv8 model, trained for two classes (gate and stop sign), runs on the ground computer on the video stream. A ROS 2 node turns detections into velocity commands through a six-state machine:

  1. Search360° scan for the next gate, including blind manoeuvres for difficult angles.
  2. AlignPID control centres the drone on the gate, compensating for the off-centre lens and for camera tilt at speed.
  3. PenetrateA short, aggressive forward burst to clear the gate.
  4. BrakeReverse thrust to stabilise before searching again.
  5. LandPrecision approach once the stop sign is detected.
  6. RecoveryKeep the last known trajectory if the target is lost.

Results

99.4%Gate detection accuracy
99.0%Stop-sign accuracy
