I'm a mechatronics engineer who spent 2.5 years keeping ships on position as a dynamic positioning service engineer at Kongsberg Maritime, and who is now finishing an M.Sc. in Robotics & Autonomous Systems at the University of Turku.

Working on DP systems showed me what real autonomy on a vessel takes: redundant sensors, careful state estimation, controllers that have to work every time, and long days of testing with the client watching. My Master's gave me the tools to build those systems myself, and I've used them on a real BlueBoat, in an Unreal Engine simulator, on FPGA hardware and on drones.

I'm looking for roles in robotics and autonomy, marine and industrial automation, commissioning and field engineering, or simulation in Finland.
